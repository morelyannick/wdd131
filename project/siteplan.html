African Cuisine Restaurant - Site Plan

Site Name

African Cuisine Restaurant

This name clearly represents the purpose of the website. It focuses on African dishes, traditional flavors, and restaurant experiences.

Site Purpose

This website will present African dishes, menu ideas, ingredients, and information about African food culture. It will include images, dish descriptions, and restaurant information.

Reason for Choosing This Topic

I chose this subject because I am interested in starting a business in the food industry and opening a restaurant focused on African cuisine. This topic is meaningful to me and enjoyable to build into a website.

Scenarios

  • What traditional African dishes are available on the menu?
  • What ingredients are used to prepare these meals?
  • Where can I find restaurant contact information?
  • Can I learn about the cultural background of these dishes?

Color Schema

Terracotta Orange (#C75C2A) → headings, buttons, accents

Cream (#F8F1E5) → page background

Deep Green (#4F6D4A) → accents and highlights

Dark Brown (#2E2E2E) → text

Typography

  • Playfair Display → headings and titles
  • Poppins → body text and paragraphs
